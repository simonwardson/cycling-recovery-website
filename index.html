About

Cycling Recovery Tracker is a personal application designed to combine Garmin cycling activity data with Oura sleep and recovery information.

Its purpose is to analyse training load, endurance performance, sleep, heart rate variability and recovery trends.

The application is intended for personal use and is not offered as a public service.

Privacy Policy

The application requests authorised access to selected Oura data, including sleep, readiness, heart rate and activity information.

Authorised data is processed for personal training and recovery analysis and may be stored in private Google Cloud resources.

Personal health data and authentication credentials are not published through this website or its public GitHub repository.

Data is not sold or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked through the Oura account's connected applications settings. Stored data can be deleted by the application owner.

This policy will be updated if the application's data handling practices change.

Terms of Service
